Before any Purchase activity can be performed, the user must be authenticated against the HMX server. Each successful login establishes a session bound to the user account, the active company, and the active branch. Logging out terminates the current session and returns the user to the login screen.

1.1 Logging In

Authentication is required to access the Purchase module and any of its data. Use the credentials issued by your system administrator. If your password has been forgotten, click Forgot & Reset Password? on the login form to start the recovery flow.

Field Reference

The login form on the database-specific page exposes only two fields:

Field NameData TypeMandatoryDescription & Function
UsernameTextYESThe login identifier of the user. Either the user's login name or the e-mail address registered on the User master, depending on the server configuration.
PasswordPasswordYESSecret credential associated with the username. Case-sensitive. Use the Forgot & Reset Password? link on the login form when the password is not known.

Procedure

To log in to the Purchase module, follow these steps:

  1. Open a web browser and navigate to the HMX login page provided by your system administrator. The page shows an empty Username and Password form.

  2. Enter your Username in the first field and your Password in the second field. Both fields are mandatory. Passwords are masked as you type.

    Common pitfall: The username and password are case-sensitive. If you receive an authentication error, verify Caps Lock is off and that no leading or trailing whitespace was pasted into either field.

Logging out terminates the current session and clears the session cookie from the browser. Any unsaved form changes will be lost — save or discard pending work before logging out.

1.1 Purchase Settings

Purchase ManagerBuyerConfiguration

Overview

Use Purchase Settings to set the baseline rules for vendor codes, credit-limit control, PO revision, document expiry, receiving, and bill validation. Treat this page as the first checkpoint before Purchase transactions go live.

Prerequisites

  • You can open the Purchase configuration menus.
  • Confirm the company policy for approvals, numbering, receiving, billing, and integrations before changing the settings.
  • After changing a setting, test it with a sample Purchase document before rolling it out to users.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Open Purchase > Settings & Rules > Purchase Settings before users start creating live purchase documents.

  2. Review the vendor sequence mode, then decide whether Vendor Code should be generated by the system or entered manually by users.

  3. Set the credit-limit rule so buyers either receive a warning or are blocked when a vendor exceeds the approved limit.

  4. Turn Purchase Order revision on only when the company allows controlled changes after a PO has been issued.

  5. Configure Draft PO Numbering and Revised Document – Sequence Numbering when the company requires its own draft numbering or a separate sequence for revised documents.

  6. Set the document expiry periods on the Orders tab: Purchase Request Expiry Period Days, RFQ Expiry Period Days, and Purchase Tender Expiry Period Days. Documents that pass the configured number of days can expire automatically, so set the period based on the team’s follow-up policy.

  7. Choose how receiving should be validated so Purchase Orders and Receiving Notes follow the company’s operational rules.

  8. Purchase Settings are stored per company. Switch the active company in the top-left menu before editing, because values saved for one company do not apply to another.

  9. On the Bill tab, set the three-way matching behaviour: 3-Way Matching Mode, Unit Price Tolerance, and Quantity Tolerance control how far a bill may deviate from the purchase order and the receipt before it is blocked.

1.5 Approval, Sequence & Mail Templates

Overview

This section explains the governance setup behind Purchase approvals, document numbering, RFQ emails, scheduled automation, and Blanket Order approval. Administrators usually prepare these settings before buyers and requesters create Purchase Request, RFQ, Direct Purchase, Blanket Order, Vendor Pricelist, KPI Target, or related documents.

Some rules are already active in the system even though they are not shown as editable fields on the Purchase setup screen. Because of that, this manual separates three things: what users can check in the UI, what is already active in runtime, and what should not be configured manually from a screen that is not available.

Prerequisites

PrerequisiteWhy It Matters
Administrator or authorized Purchase configuration accessThese settings control governance, so not every buyer or requester will see them.
Relevant Purchase document types are availableApproval and numbering can behave differently for Purchase Request, RFQ, Direct Purchase, Purchase Order, Blanket Order, Vendor Pricelist, and KPI Target.
Approval Matrix and rules are configuredA document enters approval only when an active workflow and matching approval rule are available.
Mail templates and communication channels are configuredRFQ emails and reminders need valid recipients, templates, attachments, and activities.
Scheduled jobs are reviewed by technical or administrator usersSome automation, such as blanket order transition or expired pricelist checks, runs from backend schedules and is not operated directly from the Purchase document screen.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Review the Purchase approval governance reference and confirm which Purchase documents are controlled by approval workflows. Use this as the starting point before documenting or testing any specific approval path.
  2. Open a Purchase document that requires approval, then verify that the expected approval action and status are available. In the verified UI, operational approval can be checked from documents such as Purchase Request, Vendor, Vendor Pricelist, KPI Target, or related Purchase records that show Submit for Approval, Waiting for Approval, Approve, Reject, or Approval History.
  3. After submission, confirm that the document status changes to Waiting for Approval and that approver actions are available only to authorized users. This confirms that the approval rule is not only present in configuration, but also affects the document workflow at runtime.
  4. Review Order Type sequence behavior from the Order Type configuration. The visible Sequence tab supports the document numbering previews exposed in the UI. Direct Purchase sequence exists in source and is used by backend sequence logic, but the verified Order Type UI does not expose a separate Direct Purchase Sequence field or preview, so users should not be instructed to configure it manually from this screen.
  5. Review RFQ communication from the RFQ record. Use the communication history, attachments, and available send actions to verify that vendor communication follows the configured RFQ mail template and that the user can trace messages sent from the document.
  6. Review scheduled automation through the records affected by the automation. Source and Master Demo runtime show Blanket Order automatic status transition and expired pricelist checks as enabled, while some other automation, such as Vendor Performance Scoring and Peppol Purchase Order Status refresh, may be disabled in the verified runtime. Operational users should validate the outcome on related records instead of changing backend schedules from the Purchase document screen.
  7. Verify Blanket Order approval separately when Blanket Order is used. Source configuration includes Blanket Order approval workflow and approval rule, and Master Demo runtime verification shows the Purchase Blanket Order workflow and Approval Blanket Order rule as active. Users should still follow the visible document actions in the Blanket Order screen and should not change approval state directly from backend configuration.
  8. Review the Approval History after an approval action is completed or rejected. Approval History is the traceability layer that confirms who acted, when the action happened, and whether the record is ready for the next Purchase process.

5 Partisipasi Tender & Lelang Elektronik

5.1 Pencarian Peluang Tender Terbuka

5.2 Pengajuan Penawaran Harga (Submit Quotation)

  1. Eksplorasi Tender: Masuk ke menu Tender di sidebar navigasi. Gunakan tab filter status untuk menyortir lelang:

    • All Tenders: Menampilkan seluruh arsip peluang lelang pengadaan.
    • Open: Tender pengadaan yang sedang membuka pengajuan penawaran harga.
    • Submitted: Tender di mana vendor telah mengajukan penawaran dan sedang menunggu batas penutupan.
    • Under Evaluation: Tender yang telah ditutup dan sedang dinilai oleh Tim Komite Pengadaan Company.
    • Awarded: Tender yang telah dimenangkan oleh perusahaan Anda.

  2. Telaah Dokumen Tender & Pra-Kualifikasi: Klik salah satu kartu tender (misal: TND/2026/10/001) untuk membuka rincian spesifikasi lelang.   3. Pengajuan Penawaran Harga Bid (4-Step Quotation Wizard): Klik tombol Submit Quotation. Isi wizard komersial bertahap:

    • Step 1: Quotation Info: Nomor referensi penawaran internal vendor, masa berlaku penawaran, dan catatan pengiriman.
    • Step 2: Technical Compliance: Konfirmasi kesesuaian merek, model spesifikasi teknis, dan sertifikat garansi purnajual.
    • Step 3: Commercial Pricing: Masukkan harga satuan penawaran per item baris, diskon kuantitas, dan tarif pajak (PPN).
    • Step 4: Documents Upload: Unggah dokumen proposal teknis dan surat dukungan keagenan resmi.

  4. Pengumuman & Konfirmasi Pemenang (Award Acceptance): Saat proses evaluasi selesai dan penawaran Anda terpilih sebagai pemenang tender, portal menampilkan spanduk ucapan selamat resmi (Congratulations! 9 Penanganan Kendala Operasional (Troubleshooting)

9.1 Diagnosa Masalah & Langkah Penyelesaian

Mengapa akun saya masih berstatus Pending Approval dan tidak dapat mengajukan bid tender?
Setelah pengajuan registrasi, tim legal dan kepatuhan pengadaan Company melakukan verifikasi keabsahan NPWP, NIB, dan rekening bank. Proses peninjauan membutuhkan 1–2 hari kerja. Jika mendesak, hubungi buyer terkait melalui email kontak dukungan pengadaan.
Tombol Submit Quotation pada lelang tender tidak dapat diklik atau berwarna abu-abu (disabled).
Pastikan tender belum melewati tanggal batas akhir (Deadline). Periksa juga apakah kategori bidang usaha akun vendor Anda sesuai dengan cakupan lelang yang disyaratkan. Jika ada dokumen wajib pra-kualifikasi yang belum diunggah, lengkapi berkas tersebut terlebih dahulu.
Format berkas apa saja yang didukung untuk unggah dokumen legalitas dan proposal tender?
Portal mendukung berkas berformat PDF, JPG, JPEG, dan PNG dengan ukuran maksimum 10 MB per berkas. Pastikan dokumen pindaian terbaca jelas dan tidak diproteksi kata sandi.
Bagaimana cara mengubah nomor rekening bank penerimaan pembayaran?
Masuk ke menu Profile di bilah navigasi kiri, pilih tab Rekening Bank, lalu ajukan perubahan data perbankan dilengkapi bukti buku tabungan resmi terbaru untuk diapprove kembali oleh tim Finance Company.
